FirstFunds is now a full Next.js web app, in firstfunds.zip, built around your four features. It deploys to Vercel as-is, and adding Supabase turns on sign-in and saved progress. I also updated the hosted FirstFunds page you had with the same four features. All 32 tests pass, and I clicked through every feature in a browser on desktop and phone sizes with no errors.

The four features:

Financial Advisor: a money snapshot page with next steps based on your numbers, plus the paycheck breakdown and credit card simulator as tabs.
Tax Assistant: the fewest-questions tax flow from before, now with a “Save across devices” option.
Financial Advisory Academy (new, marked “Only on FirstFunds” on the home page): 10 short lessons in 3 modules covering W-2s, Social Security and Medicare, withholding, filing status, the standard deduction, tax brackets, whether your parents can claim you, deadlines, side gigs, and IRS scams.
Each lesson has short cards, most have a hands-on example using the same tax math as the Tax Assistant, and all end with a quiz.
You earn XP and badges, and every lesson links to IRS.gov.
A test checks that the dollar amounts in the lessons match the tax engine, so they can’t drift apart.
FinPilot: the tutor, renamed. It now also answers questions about using the app, like “How do I upload my W-2?”, “Can I delete my data?” and “Does FirstFunds file my taxes?”. It can explain any Academy lesson further, and the “Ask FinPilot” button is on every page.

Vercel: import the repo and deploy; no setup is needed. Open /api/health after deploying to see which keys are active. I added time limits and per-visitor rate limits on the Gemini routes so a public site can’t run up your bill.

Supabase:

Sign-in: email sign-in links with no password, or “Continue as a guest”.
What syncs: Tax Assistant progress and Academy progress, across devices.
Deleting: people can delete their saved data from the account page.
Setup: paste the SQL file from supabase/migrations into Supabase’s SQL editor, then turn on email sign-in and add your site’s address in Supabase’s Authentication settings. The README walks through it.
Keys: Vercel’s Supabase integration sets NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_URL and NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY, and older projects use the anon key instead. The app accepts either.
